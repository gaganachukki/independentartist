FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions)
How do I cancel a booking?
You can cancel a booking from the "My Bookings" tab. Note that cancellation policies vary by artist.
When is the final payment due?
Final payments are usually due 3 days before the event date, unless otherwise specified by the artist.
Can I communicate with the artist before booking?
Yes, you can send a message through the artist's profile page to discuss details before confirming.
What if the artist doesn't show up?
We have a 100% money-back guarantee in the rare event of a no-show. Contact support immediately.
